# Prāna Wellness & Living Community

> **"Feel Better. Live Healthier. Grow Together."**  
> *Small daily habits. Better lifestyle. A healthier future.*

A premium, international fitness and wellness community web application focused exclusively on healthy lifestyle coaching, balanced natural nutrition, mindful daily habits, online live circles, and supportive community camaraderie.

---

## 🚫 Crucial Brand Identity (Strict Non-Gym Ethos)
This is **NOT** a gym website. It strictly contains:
- **Zero** gym equipment, dumbbells, barbells, or weightlifting machines.
- **Zero** aggressive bodybuilding culture or punishing workout routines.
- **Zero** gym membership plans based on iron machines.
- **100% focus on:** Gentle daily movement (mindful walks, bed-to-mat stretching), circadian sleep habits, hydration rituals, whole-food colorful nutrition, morning sunrise breathing circles, and personal wellness coaching.

---

## 🌟 Key Architecture & Features

### 1. Bilingual Architecture (English & বাংলা)
- Instant, full-page language switching with persistent storage.
- Real Bengali typography support using *Hind Siliguri* & *Playfair Display*.
- Translates navigation, buttons, forms, habits, event schedules, and legal declarations.

### 2. Wellness Programs (6 Core Paths)
1. **Healthy Lifestyle Coaching:** Daily habit improvement, goal setting, routine building, mentor check-ins.
2. **Nutrition Education:** Balanced meal education, understanding food labels, healthy eating habits, practical meal planning.
3. **Morning Wellness Sessions:** Guided morning routines, gentle joint mobility, diaphragmatic breathwork.
4. **Evening Wellness Sessions:** Twilight relaxation techniques, reflection, circadian rhythm optimization.
5. **Personal Wellness Consultation:** 1-on-1 private video assessment and customized habit blueprint.
6. **Wellness Community Circle:** Monthly community membership, peer circles, and habit challenges.

### 3. Online Live Sessions & Events
- Upcoming events with real-time countdown timer.
- Live video sessions (Google Meet / Zoom) where meeting links are protected and visible exclusively to registered attendees.
- Community weekend outdoor nature walk and tea circles.

### 4. Interactive Member Dashboard
- Daily healthy habit checklist (Gentle walk, planned healthy meal, mindfulness breath, consistent sleep, hydration goal).
- Instant habit toggles with celebration confetti feedback.
- 7-day consistency streak tracker.
- Direct "Ask Your Wellness Mentor" consultation messaging.

### 5. Robust Administrator Dashboard
- **Overview:** Total registered members, active programs, upcoming events, pending enquiries, revenue simulator.
- **Member Management:** Search by name/email, activate/deactivate members, CSV data export.
- **Program & Event Management:** Add, edit, configure pricing, upload image, cancel, or delete.
- **Content Moderation:** Review and approve community reflections before publication.
- **Website Settings:** Editable brand name, tagline, hero banner, contacts, and WhatsApp integration.

### 6. Payment Architecture (India - Razorpay Ready)
- Built-in secure Test Simulation Mode for instant checkout verification.
- Admin-configurable toggle for live Razorpay key integration.
